When a 19th named person arrives, the lowest-priority slot drops into the shadow count ("+N climbing") and their stat stays in the viewer map. Name labels: the six highest-priority climbers keep a name; the other twelve cycle a single floating name every 2s. Brick text uses the gift name, so the wall carries more identities than the sprites do.

Viewer record, per user, for the session:
- display name, total coins, last gift time, last follow time, bricks painted, prestige badges (how many collapses they were present for)
- no permanent account, no cross-stream database required for v1

Listener duties, and only these:
- connect with the streamer's uniqueId
- on disconnect, backoff 2s, 5s, 15s, then 30s, and keep trying
- emit normalized events: { id, type, userId, name, coins, giftName, at }
- type is follow | like | share | gift | chat
- dedupe by TikTok event id
- coalesce same-user gifts inside 1.5s before emit
- batch likes and emit every 2s as a count
- ignore anything the sim does not use (emotes, room stats, viewer-count spam)

Browser duties:
- render and simulate even if the socket is down; the climb never waits on chat
- on reconnect, keep going; missed events are missed, and that is acceptable
- write spire-state.json every 20s: floor, lava, checkpoint, prestige, biome, boss, viewer map, last 40 bricks, ticker
- on load, resume that file if it is under 12 hours old; otherwise start a fresh spire at floor 1 with gap 12
- if the animation frame stalls for more than 5s, reload the page; OBS browser sources survive a reload, and the json brings the floor back

Process model for a 6 hour leave:
- listener and a tiny static file server launched by one start script
- OBS already open on the scene
- Windows power settings: prevent sleep on AC
- a one-line on-screen "LIVE LINK OK / RECONNECTING" dot in the top margin, 16px, so a glance at the phone preview shows the pipe without a host talking

Failure behavior the sim must swallow without a human:
- duplicate gift event: second id dropped
- gift with coins 0: treat as a follow-level join, no hop
- name longer than 18 characters: slice
- missing display name: "climber"
- boss timer and hazard timer are timestamps, not frame counters, so a hitch does not skip or double a boss
- snapshot writes are atomic (write temp, rename)

BUILD ORDER

1. Fake-gift canvas. Floor, lava, gap, 18 slots, ticker, legend, keyboard keys 1-6 fire the six bands. No network. The screen has to read in three seconds before TikTok is involved.
2. Rubber band, hazards, boss, collapse, prestige. Leave it running two hours on fake input and on zero input.
3. Node listener plus Socket.IO, still with a local fake emitter, then the real connector against a live room.
4. Streak coalesce, like batching, shadow count, brick wall, hourly reel.
5. Biome palettes and the six event animations. Juice after the rates feel fair.
6. Soak: six hours, socket dropped twice on purpose, page reloaded once, state resumes, memory flat.

V1 DOES NOT INCLUDE

- personal weapons, inventories, or a winner
- any gift that stuns other viewers or deletes their bricks
- a second game mode
- accounts, logins, or a database beyond spire-state.json
- the streamer clicking, talking, or reading donations aloud

RATES TO TUNE AFTER A REAL ROOM, NOT BEFORE

The log curve, the 18 slot cap, the 40s hazard, and the 50-floor boss are the knobs. First watch a quiet hour and confirm the gap still produces a close call. Then watch a rose flood and confirm hops stay readable. Then watch one landmark and confirm the floor counter barely jumps while the screen clearly belongs to that person for a few seconds.

SPRITE LIST

The stream is watched on a phone, then crushed by TikTok's video encoder. Sprites are big shapes, 2px outlines, and flat color. Fine detail, profile photos, and thin lines disappear, so they are not part of the look.

One character. Every viewer is the same soot-kid body. A person is recognizable by name, a hashed body color, a hat, and a prop. Eighteen different species would turn the shaft into noise.

V1 can draw every sprite in code at the sizes below (rounded rects, circles, triangles). PNG sheets replace those drawings later without moving layout. Same grid either way.

Grid: design at 32px, draw bodies at 2x (64px on the 1080-wide canvas). Nearest-neighbor only. Animation is 2 frames at 4 fps for anything that loops. Bursts are allowed to be 8 frames, then they return to the loop. Particle cap stays 30.

Draw order, back to front: cape, body, hand prop, hat or crown, 2px outline, name plate.

IDEAS (parked, the rules above still hold)

These reuse the same sheets. They are optional juice, in the order they help a mute phone stream.

1. Gap numeral. Under gap 5, hide the small HUD number and put a 72px "9" on the shaft. People understand a countdown even when they do not understand the game.

2. Collapse as a tour. During the 8s fall, the camera follows the pack down the brick wall they already painted, slip frame on everyone, then the checkpoint notch stamps. The failure is a replay of the room's names.

3. Landmark intro is bricks, not a new character. For 2.5s, brick_landmark plates stack in the margin spelling the name, the comet prop drops out of the last plate, and the cape settles. Then weather (fx_shard, biome accent) runs 45s.

4. Headbutt boss. One hop frame plus boss_hit is the whole fight. A HP bar in the top HUD is the rest. Easy to read, cheap to run for 90s.

5. Regulars. The third gift in a session adds a 6px gold dot on that name plate, code-only. Whales already have crowns. The dot is for people who came back with roses.

6. Sinking memory. Dim bricks under the lava are the emotional prop. A scroller who arrives during a high floor still sees a column of names disappearing into the glow.

7. Costume hazards later. Keep the six behavior ids. Redraw haz_rock as a pinecone in forest, an icicle in ice, a laser tick in neon. The surge math does not change, the screenshot does, every 100 floors.

8. Prop overrides for gifts the room actually sends. If giftName contains "rose", band 1 draws a rose instead of prop_bud. If it contains "doughnut", band 3 draws a doughnut instead of prop_crate. Everything else stays on the band prop. The catalog can change without a new system.

9. Join wave. A follow plays climber_hop once and gets no brick. The hop is how the room sees a free join. A gift hop is the same frame plus a brick, so the upgrade is obvious.

10. Hourly reel. Twelve seconds of the camera drifting down the landmark plaques only, names at 40px, then a cut back to the live pack. No new art.
